Our story

How a group of women in Meli Nane turned home cooking into a shared livelihood.

How it began

Culture of Mamas began in Meli Nane, a village in Bububu north of Stone Town, with Maskat Shineni. As a divorced woman she faced stigma, and she set out to challenge it by turning her cooking into an income.

Other women joined her, and together they welcomed visitors who wanted to learn real Zanzibari home cooking.

Today about 15 women benefit directly or indirectly. The income helps pay school fees and support families, and the group also backs a local boys’ football team.
