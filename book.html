Packages and prices

Prices are in US dollars. Need a ride? Return transfers from Stone Town can be added to any package from $25.

Day experiences

Most popular

Mama’s Kitchen

$85 per adult

Our signature day. You are welcomed with song, learn the dishes and spices, cook side by side with the mamas in a kijungu clay pot and sit down to share the meal, finished with spiced coffee.

  • About 4 hours
  • Children 4–11: $25 · under 4 free
  • Vegetarian friendly

Stone Town & the Mamas

$159 per person

Morning walk through the lanes and history of Stone Town with a local guide, then the drive north to Bububu to cook and eat with the mamas.

  • About 7 hours
  • Return transfers included

Karibu Lunch

$79 per person

No cooking, just a warm welcome. Dress in Zanzibari kanga and kofia and enjoy a home-cooked Swahili lunch with fresh juice and local snacks.

  • About 3 hours
  • Return transfer from Stone Town included

Mama’s Table Lunch

$45 per adult

A relaxed home-style Swahili lunch of six dishes, cooked by the mamas and served family style.

  • 1 PM – 3 PM
  • Children 4–11: $15 · under 4 free

Coconut Oil Workshop

$45 per person

Crack, grate and press fresh coconuts the traditional way and make your own coconut oil to take home.

  • About 3 hours
  • Take-home bottle of your oil
