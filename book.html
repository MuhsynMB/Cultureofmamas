Packages and prices

Signature experience

Cooking & Culture Experience

$60 per person

Our signature day. You are welcomed with song, learn the dishes and spices, cook side by side with the mamas in a kijungu clay pot and sit down to share the meal, finished with spiced coffee.

  • About 4 hours
  • Vegetarian friendly

Henna Drawing

$15 per person

Have traditional Zanzibari henna designs drawn on your hands by the mamas, a custom for weddings, Eid and celebrations across the island.

  • Traditional hand designs

Ukili Palm Leaf Weaving

$26 per person

Learn ukili, the Zanzibari craft of weaving dried palm leaves into colourful strips for mats, baskets and hats, taught by the mamas.

  • Traditional Swahili craft
