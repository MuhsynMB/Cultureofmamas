A Zanzibar cooking class with song, spice & the mamas

Zanzibar Cooking & Culture

A women-led Swahili cooking class and culture experience in a village home in Meli Nane, Bububu, just 10 km north of Stone Town. Learn the dishes, share the table and see Zanzibar beyond the resorts.

Your day with the mamas

The experience takes about four hours. Vegetarian guests are welcome.

Come hungry. You will leave full.

  1. Arrive and be welcomedMeet the mamas with singing and dancing at the house with the red gate.
  2. Learn the dishesSee the ingredients and spices, and hear how each recipe is made.
  3. Cook togetherPrepare the meal by hand, side by side with the mamas.
  4. Share the mealSit down to eat what you cooked, with spiced coffee to finish.

Led by women, for the community

Culture of Mamas started as a way for Zanzibari women to turn their cooking and culture into a livelihood.

Every booking supports the women who host you and their families.

When you cook with the mamas, you are not a guest in a kitchen. You are part of the household.
